Tempo Demo Curriculum

Hands-On Tempo Classes

A one-hour-per-session teaching track for showing students how Tempo payment primitives become product workflows.

Each class has one tutorial README that runs live against Tempo testnet and that students can follow again afterward. Every script creates throwaway accounts, funds them from the testnet faucet, and shows real transactions and real failures. Nothing is simulated, and no wallet or private key is needed.

Curriculum README

Sessions

01

Transaction Controls

Sign payments with validAfter and validBefore and watch the node enforce the window, then compare one sequential nonce, parallel nonceKey lanes, and an expiring nonce whose replay is refused.

payment windows / nonce lanes / expiring nonces
02

Sponsored Payments

Pay through Tempo's public fee payer, then run the app's own relay that sponsors only what its policy allows. The chain itself rejects replays, expired approvals, and an empty sponsor.

fee payer / relay policy
03

Payment Reconciliation

Send six real payments against one invoice and let a worker read receipts and memo events to decide which one settles it, then find them all through the indexer.

memos / receipts / indexer
04

Access Keys And Agents

Give an agent its own access key with scopes, a budget, and an expiry. Watch the protocol refuse every overreach, then revoke the key and let a session key expire.

access keys / least privilege
05

Batch Payments

Validate a payroll manifest against chain data, pay it as one Tempo Transaction, and show that an overdrawing batch fails all or nothing.

batch calls / payroll
06

TIP-403 Policies

Launch a stablecoin behind a whitelist, then add a treasury receive policy that parks unwanted deposits in ReceivePolicyGuard until the recovery authority releases them.

token policy / receive policy
07

Stablecoin DEX

Quote, swap with a bound, hit real slippage and no-liquidity reverts, and pay a merchant who wants a different stablecoin in one atomic transaction.

quote tokens / slippage / atomic payment
08

Private Zones

Deposit into Zone A, sign the zone's RPC token with your own key, pay privately, and see exactly what each user can and cannot read.

zone RPC tokens / privacy boundaries
09

Capstone Payment App

Combine invoices, sponsorship tied to invoice state, and reconciliation from receipts, and compare the ledger with what actually arrived in the wallet.

payment product architecture
10

Fee AMM

See which token pays each fee and how the block's proposer is paid through a fixed-price swap, then watch a brand-new stablecoin get refused as a fee token until someone seeds its Fee AMM pool.

fee tokens / fee conversion
